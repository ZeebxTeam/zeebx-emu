Zeebx for iPhone, through AltStore

The file is zeebx-ios.ipa, next to this text. It is the device app,
unsigned. Classic AltStore signs it with your Apple ID.
AltStore PAL only installs packages notarized by Apple, and this IPA isn't one.

The game goes in Documents/roms, inside the app. A loose .mod works,
and so does a .zip or a .7z. The iPhone needs iOS 16 or newer.

1. On the Mac, download AltServer from https://altstore.io and open it. It
   lives in the menu bar.
2. Plug the iPhone in with the cable, unlock it and tap Trust This Computer.
3. In the AltServer menu, choose Install AltStore and the iPhone. Sign in
   with the Apple ID and password. Apple sends a code.
4. On the iPhone: Settings, General, VPN & Device Management. Trust the
   certificate of that Apple ID.
5. Settings, Privacy & Security, Developer Mode. The phone
   restarts.
6. Open AltStore, go to My Apps, tap + and choose zeebx-ios.ipa.
   You can send it over AirDrop, through the Files app, or from AltServer
   with Sideload .ipa.

With a free Apple ID the signature lasts 7 days and there is room for
about 3 apps. AltStore takes one slot. With AltServer open and the iPhone
on the same Wi-Fi network, AltStore renews it. Refresh All does the same
right away. Renewing does not erase the save.

The game: open Zeebx once. Plug the iPhone into the Mac, open the Zeebx
tab in Finder and put the .zip or the .mod inside roms. In the app, tap
Refresh. If Finder writes the file with 0 bytes, delete it and copy it
through the iPhone's Files app (On My iPhone, Zeebx, roms).
