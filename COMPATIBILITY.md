# Notes:

- It is recommended to configure the controller when opening the emulator for the first time; even so, some games may have different inputs.
- Games compatible with the Boomerang (motion sensor), such as Crash Nitro Kart 3D, all the Zeebo Sports titles, among others, can be played using controllers with motion sensors - such as DualShock 4, DualSense, Wii Remote and Switch Pro Controller - through Bluetooth pairing. This support for sensor controllers over Bluetooth currently applies to desktop Linux; in the Libretro cores and on the other platforms the motion sensor is not supported yet ([#60](https://github.com/ZeebxTeam/zeebx-emu/issues/60)).
- All the Data East games are arcade games running in their own built-in emulator.
- No game, whether official, homebrew or port, is free of bugs or crashes, and some may still be incompatible. Thanks for the reports!
- Zeeboids online features may not be running at all times, since it is still a feature in development. All Zeeboids avatars may be deleted after development and migration to a proper final server, but feel free to test it.
